Energy
in dance

The same movement can feel completely different depending on the feeling / effort you give it.

01 / WEIGHT

Strong

or light

Is the movement light, floaty and soft or does it feel stronger, powerful and weighted.

02 / FLOW

Flow

Is the movement free to move within the space or is it bound to constrain such as a body part or area.

03 / TIME

Sustained

or sudden

What is the path the movement takes, this may be a sudden change or one movement prolonged for a greater period of time.
